Who We Are

POWER MARINE SERVICES INC. is a registered Liberian commercial diving and maritime logistics firm headquartered on Somalia Drive in Monrovia, Liberia. We deliver rapid, dependable subsea solutions to international cargo vessels, tankers, offshore rigs, and port operators across Liberia. You can look at our gallery to see most of our work or contact for more information.

Guided by our core motto — “Honesty, Hard – Work & Discipline” — our certified commercial divers and marine technicians operate 24/7 across the Freeport of Monrovia, Grand Bassa Port, and Sinoe Port to minimize vessel turnaround times and ensure full regulatory compliance.
