Who We Are

POWER MARINE SERVICES INC. is a registered Liberian commercial diving and maritime logistics firm headquartered in Dixville, Bassa town, Barnersville, Monrovia, Liberia. We deliver rapid, dependable subsea solutions to international cargo vessels, tankers, offshore rigs, and port operators across Liberia. You can look at our gallery to see most of our work or contact us for more information.

Guided by our core motto — “Honesty, Hard – Work & Discipline” — our certified commercial divers and marine technicians operate 24/7 across the Freeport of Monrovia, Grand Bassa Port, and Sinoe Port to minimize vessel turnaround times and ensure full regulatory compliance.

Every dive operation follows strict international commercial diving safety protocols, equipped with surface-supplied air systems, redundant communication lines, and standby rescue divers.
